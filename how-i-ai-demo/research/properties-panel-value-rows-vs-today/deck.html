Round 2: rows that look like controls

Each value sits in a field with a chevron, so a row reads as something you can change.

Round 2: rows stay open

Open as many rows as you use. They stay open as the selection changes, and the panel remembers them.

Round 2: names that say what they do

Look is now Quick style, with one line on what it sets. Solid, Hatched and Cross-hatched are now Filled, Lines and Crossed lines.
